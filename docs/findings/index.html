How to read this

The archive is a MAP-Elites structure: one grid per algebraic order 1 to 4, whose cells are keyed by (stage count, cycle bucket). A cell holds at most one record, its elite: the verified tableau with the lowest held-out error seen so far for that shape and cost; ties keep the earlier record.

Stage rows span 2 to 6 by default, extended with a row for any other stage count that holds an elite (order 1's single-stage cell appears as s=1), and the cycle buckets are log2-spaced bins of the m0plus_fast cycles per step: bucket 0 means fewer than 16 cycles, bucket 1 is 16 to 31, bucket 2 is 32 to 63, doubling each column, and bucket 7 collects everything at 1024 cycles or more.

The blue ramp is scaled within each grid, so shades compare cells of the same order only. An empty cell means no verified tableau has landed there yet. Click any filled cell to open its detail page.

In the tables below, each row is one cell's elite and its hash links to the detail page. A large gap between search_error and heldout_error is overfitting to the visible search set; measured_order, the tier badge and verifier_hash are defined in the glossary.
